# Darcy mass balance, two blocks side by side (plan 11 stage 3b)

`mass_balance_1d` (plan 07 gate 4.1(b2): 100 cells along x, face 1 sealed, face 2 open, a constant source, `tridiag`)
with four identical lines along j instead of two, cut into 1 × 2 blocks of 100 × 2 cells side by side. Both blocks
solve line-implicitly along x and form one correction group (plan 10 D22) whose blocks share every line position: the
group's summed line adds the two blocks' partial sums at each position. That is the case where Group_Corrections' sums
(per block, then the blocks in order; plan 11 stage 3b) round differently from the running sum before it, and where the
N-rank result must still equal the 1-rank one.
