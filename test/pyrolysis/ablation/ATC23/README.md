## The case

It is ATC 2.2 (`../ATC22/README.md`) with h_r = 25 MJ/kg on the two heating rows of the env table (ATC2 v2.8; no
shipped PATO variant exists, so `edit_pato_atc2x.py --case 2.3` makes the same edit in PATO's `BoundaryConditions`).

The virgin-98 % front misses the 1.5× target by 15 %. Two reasons:
- PATO and Amaryllis agree unusually well there (0.148 mm);
- the front sits in cells 0.33 mm wide, so a front error below one cell is what a first-order scheme gives.

The target is recorded, not gated (P-D4).

**Where the largest T difference comes from.** It is the early thermal wave near the surface, which neither code has
converged at 100 cells. Measured over 0–8 s, both codes at N400 (the PATO run is in `ref/mesh-N400/`):

| depth | PATO N400 − N100 | FUSS N400 − N100 | FUSS − PATO, N100 | FUSS − PATO, N400 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 mm | −26.90 K | −12.45 K | −10.97 K | +12.66 K |
| 2 mm | −14.23 K | −8.22 K | +3.44 K | +5.69 K |
| 4 mm | −6.86 K | −8.10 K | +6.62 K | +3.26 K |
| 8 mm | −1.85 K | −6.23 K | +6.89 K | +2.28 K |

All entries are maxima over t. The 1 mm transient of the first second is still moving in both codes at 400 cells: PATO by
27 K and FUSS by 12 K from N100 to N400, and they stay 12.7 K apart at N400. So no agreement is claimed there. From 4 mm down
the codes agree to 3.3 K at N400.

**R2.** e_front = 0.254 mm at 57.2 s against a 0.331 mm front cell (front cells 0.133–0.516 mm over the run): first order
suffices, as on 2.2.
