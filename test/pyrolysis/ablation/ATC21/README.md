## The oracle (`ref/`, see `ref/README.md`)

The oracle is PATO 3.1 on the same problem. Edits: `edit_pato_atc2x.py --case 2.1 --max-dt 0.0005`, i.e. `movingMesh no`, the
DIC preconditioners a static mesh requires, and a 0.5 ms maximum step instead of the tutorial's 5 ms.

**Why the smaller step.** At 5 ms PATO's own time error dominates the comparison. Measured 2026-10-13 on the same
100-cell mesh in both codes:

| 0.2 | 1009.98 | 1025.47 | 1027.03 | 1028.59 | 1028.67 | +0.08 | −0.05 |
| 0.5 | 1163.86 | 1174.36 | 1175.13 | 1175.90 | 1176.03 | +0.13 | −0.38 |
| 1 | 1274.76 | 1282.22 | 1282.70 | 1283.18 | 1283.25 | +0.07 | −0.17 |
| 30 | 1587.22 | 1587.30 | 1587.30 | 1587.30 | 1587.33 | +0.03 | +0.09 |

How the table was made:
- **0.25 ms\*** is run with the Ta solve tolerance at 1e-12. At the shipped 1e-6, 30035 of its Ta solves did zero iterations
  between 40 and 60 s, so T was not advanced, and the run drifted 38 K. With 1e-12, none of its 480094 Ta and p solves did.
- **"Extrapolated"** is first-order Richardson extrapolation (PATO is implicit Euler): 2 · (0.25 ms) − (0.5 ms).
- **FUSS** is this case with field outputs every 0.02 s, so that its T_w is not interpolated across 0.1 s.
- The column T(1 mm) is the same difference at 1 mm.

On the same mesh, FUSS and the time-converged PATO agree to 0.13 K at the wall and 0.54 K at 1 mm through the heating and
the cool-down. The 5 ms oracle is 24 K warmer 0.2 s after the switch-off and 19 K cooler at t = 0.2 s.

FUSS's own mesh error, from N100 to N400 (same grading, 0.02 s sampling):
- at the wall, at most 0.55 K;
- at 1 mm, 3.25 K at 0.2 s (the thermal front is arriving), 1.1 K at 0.5 s and below 1 K afterwards.

`ref/pato-dt0.005/` and `ref/pato-dt0.00025-tight/` keep the other two PATO runs. The table can be recomputed from the
committed files.

## The gate (`verify.py`, `expected.json`; plan 08 P-D4)

FUSS is sampled on PATO's 0.1 s grid:
- T at the 7 depths from the bracketing index probes (probes.json; written at multiples of 0.1 s);
- T_w and ṁ_g from `seb.txt` (one row per solution output, about 0.1 s apart and drifting against the grid). A grid time
  whose two FUSS samples straddle a breakpoint of the env table (60 and 60.1 s) is skipped. Linear interpolation across the
  switch-off reads 72 K of sampling error into a wall that agrees to 0.1 K just before it. Elsewhere in the first second the
  interpolation still errs by up to about 4 K;
- the virgin-98 % and char-2 % fronts from the field files, by PATO's rule (`massIOModel.C`).

Against the 0.5 ms oracle, measured 2026-10-13:

| quantity | max \|FUSS − PATO\| | at | budget (measured + 2 K, or + 10 % of PATO's peak) | 1.5× target (spread PATO − Amaryllis) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T, 7 depths | 0.87 K | 60.6 s, 1 mm | 2.87 K | 38.0 K: met |
| T_w | 1.37 K | 60.5 s | 3.37 K | 148 K: met |
| ṁ_g | 2.3e-4 kg/m²/s (0.9 % of the peak) | 0.2 s | 2.83e-3 | 3.5e-3: met |
| virgin-98 % front | 11.5 µm | 0.5 s | 2.09 mm | 0.17 mm: met |
| char-2 % front | 3.5 µm | 0.2 s | 0.25 mm | 0.096 mm: met |

The verify also checks:
- (static) ṁ_ch, B′_ch, the recession and its rate are exactly 0;
- (seb) §3.2 on all 2368 rows: |F|/max term ≤ 9.9e-9, |β − ζ/(e^ζ − 1)| ≤ 7e-15, B′_pg and ṁ_ch identities exact.

The Amaryllis 2.1 files are the ATC organisers' reference (`ref/`); the 1.5× targets are recorded and do not gate.

Run with:

```bash
./FUSS.sh -p 1 solve && python3 verify.py
```

or with `run_physics_gates.sh -c ablation_atc21`. `FUSS_GATE_SELFTEST=1` sets the budgets to 0 and makes the gate fail.
`FUSS_PATO_ATC21_OUT=<PATO output/porousMat>` compares against another PATO run.
