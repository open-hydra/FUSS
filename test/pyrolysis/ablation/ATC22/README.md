## The gate (`verify.py` → `../../tools/atc2x_receding.py`, `expected.json`; plan 08 P-D4)

FUSS is sampled on PATO's 0.1 s grid (decision X-D4):
- **T at 1, 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24 mm below the initial surface.** Each field file gives T linearly between the two current cell
  centres that bracket the point, then linearly in t.
- **When a depth is compared.** Only while both codes still have it. PATO writes 0 once its surface passes the point;
  Amaryllis writes 0, or a stray value below 1 K. FUSS stops once the point lies in the last half cell, which no two centres
  bracket.
  - Why the half-cell mask: inside the surface cell, each code's interpolation rule decides the value, not the solution.
  - PATO's `cellPoint` probe tracks the cell-centre value. At 1 mm, 26 µm under the surface: PATO 1445.5 K, FUSS's
    surface cell 1442.9 K, FUSS's centre-to-T_w line 1459.5 K.
  - A centre-to-wall line therefore read 16 K of rule difference in the last 0.3 s before the 1 mm point was consumed.
- **T_w, ṁ_g, ṁ_c** from `seb.txt`. **The recession and the virgin-98 % / char-2 % fronts** from the field files (fronts
  by PATO's rule, `massIOModel.C`; depth from the initial surface).
- **Skipped grid times.** A grid time whose two FUSS samples straddle a breakpoint of the env table (60 and 60.1 s) is
  skipped for every quantity. Across the switch-off the samples are 0.1 s apart while the wall cools 140 K. Interpolating
  across it read 56 K of sampling error into T at 12 mm, 0.13 mm under the surface at 60 s.

**Where the largest T difference comes from: mesh error near the surface, in both codes.** Measured over 0–8 s with
`make_case.py --ncells 400` and `edit_pato_atc2x.py --ncells 400 --end-time 8` (the PATO run is in `ref/mesh-N400/`):

| depth | PATO N400 − N100 | FUSS N400 − N100 | FUSS − PATO, N100 | FUSS − PATO, N400 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 mm | +8.24 K | −4.01 K | +6.06 K | +5.72 K |
| 2 mm | −2.86 K | −2.16 K | +2.70 K | +2.72 K |
| 4 mm | −1.23 K | −2.31 K | +3.27 K | +1.58 K |
| 8 mm | −0.45 K | −1.90 K | +2.43 K | +0.90 K |

All entries are maxima over t. At the 6.06 K point (1 mm, 5.2 s, 84 µm under the surface), PATO moves +8.2 K from N100 to
N400 while FUSS moves +2.7 K: the two refined answers agree to 0.5 K. From 4 mm down the codes agree to 1.6 K at N400.

**R2 (plan 08 decision 4).** e_front = max_t |x98,FUSS − x98,PATO| = 0.188 mm at 59.2 s. The FUSS cell holding the front
then is 0.265 mm wide (the front cells span 0.130–0.383 mm over the run). e_front < Δx_local, so no limited (MUSCL)
reconstruction is added. On 2.3 the same holds (`../ATC23/README.md`).

The verify also checks:
- (bal-m) the type-2 mass M_s + M_g + M_gas_out + M_abl + G_abl of `energy-budget.txt` holds to 6.1e-15 (bound 1e-12; 1.0e-14 on the stage-C binary);
- (bal-e) the energy E_tot + E_abl + Q_out − Q_src holds to 5.7e-11 (bound 5e-10). This drift is per-step round-off: it
  grows as t^0.5 and grows when the step shrinks;
- (seb) §3.2 on all 2376 rows: |F|/max term ≤ 9.7e-9, |β − ζ/(e^ζ − 1)| ≤ 1.4e-10, B′_pg and ṁ_ch identities exact.

The Amaryllis 2.2 files are the ATC organisers' reference (`ref/`). The 1.5× targets are recorded and do not gate (P-D4).
