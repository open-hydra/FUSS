# Wall smoothing: the period-4 demonstration (plan 08 §2.6, §3.6, stage D)

Generated by `generate.py` (run from this directory). It writes `w1/`, `w5/` and `w5_2blk/`: one problem run with
`[FUSS-Ablation] smoothing-width = 1` (off) and `5` (thesis Eq. 5.1).

| item | value |
|---|---|
| material, gas closure | TACOT, type 2 (the tables of `../../darcy/ATC2-chemistryOff-type2`) |
| mesh | 2-D slab 0.2 m wide × 0.05 m deep, 40 × 20 uniform cells, one block |
| top face | the ablating wall BC 306: ATC 2.2's heating rows, h_r = 1.5 MJ/kg, λ = 0.5, the equilibrium B′ table of `../tables/` |
| other faces | sides symmetry (300), bottom adiabatic (301), k faces null |
| pattern | ρ_e u_e C_H0 = 0.3 (1 + 0.1 s_i), period four faces: s_i = +1 for i mod 4 ∈ {0, 1}, −1 otherwise (`env_hi.dat`, `env_lo.dat`, alternated in `bc.txt`) |
| run | `law = nodeshift`, 20 s; about 4–8 s of wall time each; `.slow` |

**Why the mode is damped by 5.** In the oxidation plateau ṁ_ch is proportional to ρ_e u_e C_H0, so the pattern reaches the
recession rate linearly. The node average of §2.3 step 1 turns the period-4 face pattern into a period-4 node mode,
with a factor cos(π/4). On that mode the 5-point average has transfer (1 + 2 cos(π/2) + 2 cos π)/5 = −1/5.

A face checkerboard would not do: the node average maps it to exactly zero.

**The check (`verify.py`).** It reads the top-surface recession s_k = y_k(0) − y_k(20 s) at nodes k = 0..40 from each
run's last field file. It fits nodes 3..37 (the truncated stencil excluded) to m + a cos(πk/2) + b sin(πk/2).

Measured 2026-10-13:

| width | mean recession | period-4 amplitude |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3.489 mm | 0.378 mm (10.8 % of the mean) |
| 5 | 3.498 mm | 0.0808 mm |

- The amplitude ratio is **4.674**: the bound is ≥ 4.5 and the theory 5. The margin covers the nonlinear SEB response
  and the geometry feedback over 20 s.
- The width-5 mode is reversed, as the negative transfer says.

**The seam (`w5_2blk/`).** It is `w5/` cut into two blocks of 20 × 20 cells, joined by 101 faces at x = 0.1 m. The
receding surface then spans two blocks, and the smoothing windows of the nodes beside the seam must cross it, through
the surface-node map (§2.6).

Measured 2026-10-13 (the stage-D binary, the default `[FUSS-Darcy] tol = 1e-8`):

| quantity | `w5_2blk` vs `w5` | bound | with the old connection rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| surface, every node | 7.9e-12 m | 1e-10 m | 5.6e-8 m |
| T, of the maximum | 4.0e-9 | 1e-7 | 1.1e-4 |
| χ, p_pg, η_st | 2.4e-9, 9.5e-12, 1.6e-8 | — | 1.6e-4, 3.1e-6, 1.6e-4 |

On equal spacing, the multi-block lift of §2.9 (the χ ghosts, the χ-aware conduction kernel, the conservative Darcy
rows) reproduces the single block. A window truncated at the seam would move the nodes beside it by about 1e-5 m.

The two runs do not solve the pressure along the same path. The line-uniform correction below spans one block: the
one-block run corrects all 40 columns together, while each block of the two-block run corrects its own 20 and sees the
other through the lagged connection. Both stop within the tolerance, but apart; the bounds sit at that level. Without
the correction the sweeps do not depend on the cut: line Jacobi lags the neighbour column whether it lies across a
seam or not. Both runs then took the same sweep count at every step and agreed to 1.0e-15 m and T 2.9e-12.

Tightening `tol` does not help. On this slab the sweeps reach their round-off stop (`Mod_Darcy.f90`, 64 ε of the
largest term) near 1e-10 of R_ref: `tol` = 1e-10, 1e-11 and 1e-12 give the same runs. There the seam closes to
1.4e-13 m and T 3.7e-11. But a stop decided by round-off is noisy, and the restart gate's `w5` then differs by
1.7e-10 in η_st (one-digit perturbations of its restart file: up to 3.8e-10; at `tol = 1e-8`: 5.4e-12 at most).

**The Darcy line solver.** This slab's only open face is the top 306 face, so `solver = tridiag` sweeps it
line-implicitly along j. On it, the line solver as first built diverged at every time step: a face across the lines put
its whole flux on the right-hand side and nothing on the diagonal. Since 2026-10-13 such a face puts its conductance on
the diagonal (line Jacobi with the full diagonal, `Mod_Darcy.f90` `Scatter`). An additive correction removes the error
that is uniform across the lines (`Line_Uniform_Correction`). The solve takes 578–1059 sweeps a step (mean 933 over the
25 steps of `w5`; without the correction 978–2127, mean 1826).

Run with `run_physics_gates.sh -c ablation_smoothing` (all three runs). `FUSS_GATE_SELFTEST=1` asks a ratio of 1e9 and a
zero seam difference, which makes the gate fail. The restart gate runs `w5` (6 steps per half).
