# ATC 2.2, four blocks (plan 11 stage 4)

The case `../ATC22` (the Ablation Workshop's ATC 2.2: TACOT, the type-2 gas closure, the ablating wall 306 with its
B' table on face 2, law `nodeshift`) cut into 4 × 1 blocks of 25 × 2 cells along x, with `time-threshold = 10.0` and
`sol-dtime = 0.5`. Block 4 holds the receding face; on four ranks it lives on rank 3, so the face's state reaches root
only through the exchanges of stage 4 (the rates' allgather before the nodal recession, the faces' state for
`seb.txt` and `bc306-state.dat`), and the Darcy solve, the pyrolysis and the remap run across the cuts.

Why 10 s, not the source's 120 s: only a block that owns a 306 face recedes, and the source's graded mesh makes
block 4 (x from 45.7 to 50 mm) 4.27 mm thick; the surface recedes 11.9 mm by 120 s, and at t = 15.8 s block 4 is
consumed (its lines would keep less than min-thickness, 1.27 mm: error stop 2). By 10 s it has receded 1.85 mm. A
split of the receding direction must leave the receding block thick enough for the run.
