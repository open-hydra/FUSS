# Baum & Atreya charring slab, twenty blocks (plan 11 stage 3b)

The case `../full_N100` (the Baum & Atreya 2007 benchmark, type-2 gas closure, the reactions on, 100 × 2 cells) cut into
20 × 1 blocks of 5 × 2 cells along x, joined by type-101 connections. Everything else is the source's: the slab, the
302 face at x = 0 open to the gas (`open-bc-types = 302`), the kinetics, `time-threshold = 300`, the probes.

It exists so that type-2 pyrolysis runs on several MPI ranks with every exchange of stage 3 in play: the charring front
reaches cell 13 by t = 300 s, so it crosses the cuts after cells 5 and 10 and the chi halo and chig carry chi > 0 there
(cut into four blocks of 25 cells, the front stayed inside block 1, and a run without the chi halo was still identical);
the p_pg halo, the neighbour's Gamma (once a step) and h_g Gamma (every stage) carry values that change at every cut; the
twenty blocks form one correction group of blocks in a row (plan 10 D22). On four ranks blocks b and b + 1 always live on
different ranks (the greedy partition deals them out in turn).

- Block 1 holds the heated face; block 20 the back face.
- The probes keep their cells: `back` (1 100 1 1) becomes (20 5 1 1), `x20` (1 6 1 1) becomes (2 1 1 1), the others
  stay in block 1.
- Every value in `INPUT/ic.tec` is the source's own string; `input.ini` keeps the source's `[FUSS-*]` and probe sections.
