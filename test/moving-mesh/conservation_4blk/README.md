# Energy conservation on a moving mesh, four blocks (plan 11 stage 2)

The case `../conservation` (plan 05 gate 5.4) cut into 2 × 2 blocks: the same 60 × 40 mesh, the same hot spot on a
273 K plate, the same adiabatic walls and the same `prescribed` motion with `taper-to-boundary`, now as four 30 × 20
blocks joined by type-101 connections. It exists so that the moving mesh can be run on several MPI ranks: every
moving-mesh case before it was single-block.

- Blocks are numbered row-major, b = 1 + bi + 2 bj.
- The cut faces carry 200 records of type 101 (orientation `1 0 0 1`).
- Every value in `INPUT/ic.tec` is the source's own string, so the initial state is bit-identical to
  `../conservation`'s.
- `input.ini` keeps only the source's `[FUSS-*]` sections, verbatim.

## Not comparable with the unsplit case, by construction

`taper-to-boundary` tapers the motion with sin(π i/dim) on each block (`Morph_Motion_Prescribed.f90:111-115`). Here that
pins the seams too, and the interior of every block moves on its own, so the motion differs from `../conservation`'s.
Measured, for information only:
- T differs by up to 2.0 K;
- the nodes differ by up to 2.0e-3 m;
- the run ends at 237.46 s instead of 240.66 s.

Conservation must hold all the same: every swept-volume flux is internal to a block, and the 101 interfaces exchange
conduction only.

### The taper is not exactly zero on the high-index side of a block

At i = dim the factor is sin(PI·dim/dim), not 0, despite the comment at `Morph_Motion_Prescribed.f90:105-107`:
- 1.2e-16 for dim = 20;
- 5.7e-16 for dim = 30, because PI·30/30 rounds one ulp below π.

The high-side copy of a seam node is therefore displaced by at most 5.7e-16 × amp ≈ 1.1e-18 m. Over all seam nodes this
stays at least a factor 4.2 below half an ulp of the coordinate it is added to. The worst node is block 1's (30, 3, 0),
y = 0.03 m. So the displacement rounds away and the seams stay bit-identical (measured below).

It is a margin, not an identity. It would not round away where a high-index boundary sits at a coordinate of exactly 0.

## What `verify.py` checks

Run `python3 verify.py [case_dir]` after the solve.

- **Conservation.** This is `../conservation/verify.py`'s check, on every zone of `INPUT/ic.tec` and
  `OUTPUT/field.tec`. The volumes are recomputed from the nodes, zone by zone, and E = Σ h V is summed over all blocks.
  The relative imbalance must be below 1e-10.
  - That script reads zone 1 only. On this case it reports 2.4e-7 and FAILS, because block 1 exchanges heat with its
    neighbours.
- **Seams.** Every 101 record pairs a face with its neighbour's. The four corner nodes of the two faces must be written
  alike in `field.tec`. The check is on the 15 significant digits that `field.tec` carries.

## The plan-11 gate

The gate is this case at 4 MPI ranks against itself at 1 rank: `run_regression.sh -m 4 -c test/moving-mesh/conservation_4blk`
against `-m 1`. The manifests must be identical, and conservation must be ~1e-16 either way (plan 11 §2.5).

`run_regression.sh` without `-c` finds this case through its `FUSS.sh`. A full-suite manifest therefore has three rows more
than `test/baseline/manifest.txt` until the baseline is re-recorded.
