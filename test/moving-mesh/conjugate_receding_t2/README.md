# R5's type-2 variant: pyrolysis across a block connection (plan 08 §2.9 (3), stage D)

Generated by `generate.py`. It uses `../conjugate_receding`'s two-block mesh and boundary conditions (20 + 5 cells along
x, so dx2/dx1 = 4 across the 101 interface) with TACOT in both blocks, under the type-2 gas closure.

- **Heating.** Block 1's 306 face is heated, with the chemistry off, through hconv 1000 W/m²/K toward 1000 K. It recedes
  at the TEST ONLY `sdot-override` of 1e-5 m/s.
- **What crosses the interface.** The slab decomposes near the hot face, and its gas pressure reaches the interface.
  Conduction, gas mass and gas enthalpy therefore all cross the 101 faces.
- **Run.** 200 steps of about 4.5 s, with `dump-interface-flux = true`.

**The check (`verify.py`).** Face by face, it pairs each 101 face of `interface-flux.txt` with its partner at the same
time. The sum of the two sides' flows must be ≤ 1e-12 of that quantity's largest value over the run.

Measured 2026-10-13:
- q_if (conduction): 1.6e-13;
- qm_if (gas mass): exactly 0;
- qh_if (gas enthalpy): exactly 0.

The Darcy connection row is the interior face kernel on both blocks' data: the face metric (M + M_g)/2, the face tensor
from the two cells' own lagged coefficients, and both tangential stencils. Both sides therefore evaluate one expression.

As first built (plan 07), the row used the cell's own metric at a full-cell distance and re-evaluated the neighbour's
tensor at its ghost state. The two conductances then differed by the spacing ratio.

**The Darcy solve.** Its block-Jacobi coupling across the interface is slow, but it converges: 12k–28k sweeps a step,
mean 26k over the gate's 200 steps (stage-D binary, 2026-10-13). The line-uniform correction spans one block, so it
does not reach across the interface. Without the correction the case takes 13.6k–32.2k (mean 29.6k); with the plan-07
splitting (the cross flux whole on the right-hand side) it diverges in the first step.

Run with `run_physics_gates.sh -c conjugate_receding_t2`. `FUSS_GATE_SELFTEST=1` sets the bound to 0 and makes the gate
fail. The restart gate runs the case too.
